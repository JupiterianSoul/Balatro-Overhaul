# Changelog

What changed in each version. The game has the same notes in English, French and Spanish, under About > Changelog.

## 2.0

*Overhaul 2.0 (pre-release), 2026-11-08*

- **Multiplayer.** Play Balatro online with friends or against random people, casual or ranked. Parties go up to 8 players.
- **Hologram.** The new mascot. Hologram explains the new stuff the first time you see it, and you can skip it.
- **Joker Forge.** Make your own jokers, draw them and share them online.
- **The Casino.** Minigames between runs. Jimbo Jam is a rhythm game that plays your own Jukebox songs.
- **Achievements.** Over 200 achievements, plus quests in the Progression window.
- Replays of your matches, and you can watch other games live.
- Switcheroo: both players get ready, 3 second countdown, and you swap everything except lives.
- Random Deck shuffles hand scores, prices, card values and boosters. Lobbies have a Chaos slider.
- Blue Stake works differently than vanilla: shop items cost $1 more per Ante cleared and rerolls go up by $2. Higher stakes keep it, except Nova and Eclipse.
- Wild Cards can't be debuffed by Boss Blinds. Matador gives $2 for each discard during a Boss Blind.
- Stats windows for solo and multiplayer, and a Hand Optimizer that finds your best play.
- Jimbo comments on your runs.
- A performance mode if your device struggles.
- On a phone in portrait, Fill the screen puts your hand in the middle.
- The title screen tells you when there's an update and installs it.
- Crash reports are on by default. You can turn them off in Settings.
- The launcher (PC and Android) builds the Overhaul from your own Balatro and takes care of your saves.
- The whole game is in English, French and Spanish.
