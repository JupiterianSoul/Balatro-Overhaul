# Changelog

What each version brings to players. The game shows the same notes in English, French and Spanish under About > Changelog.

## 2.0

*Overhaul 2.0 (pre-release), 2026-11-08*

- **Hologram shows you around.** Hologram, the Overhaul's mascot, walks you through everything the mod adds. Skip tutorial skips both tutorials.
- **Multiplayer.** Casual and ranked rooms, parties, replays and spectating. In Switcheroo both players press Ready, a 3 second countdown runs, then you trade everything but lives.
- **Achievements and quests.** Over 200 achievements with pop-ups when you earn them, and quests in the Progression window.
- **The Casino and Cheats.** Minigames in The Casino, among them Jimbo Jam, a rhythm game that plays the songs in your Jukebox. Cheats stay off until you allow them in Play.
- **Joker Forge.** Design your own jokers in the Forge Builder, with colour swatches for card text and controller support.
- Random deck: truly random hand scores, prices, card values and boosters, with a Chaos slider in lobbies.
- Blue Stake: shop items cost $1 more for every Ante cleared and rerolls go up $2 each. Every higher stake follows this rule, except Nova and Eclipse.
- Wild Cards are immune to every Boss Blind debuff, and Matador earns $2 for every discard you use during a Boss Blind.
- A What's new window on the title screen with the full patch notes, an About screen, and Credits with a Special thanks section and a Patreon tab to help pay for the server.
- When a new version is out, the title screen offers it and installs it from the game.
- Stats and Multiplayer Stats windows.
- A performance mode with clear options you can also use one by one.
- Jimbo comments on your runs and stays on screen.
- A Hand Optimizer built on the game's own scoring.
- Shop boosters in their own colours, distinct card backs, sleeves that sit on the deck like a coat, and colour palettes that really differ.
- Custom random runs draw from almost every run rule.
- Phone in portrait: Fill the screen widens the run and puts your hand in the middle.
- A blind you already beat cannot be played again.
- The launcher for Windows, macOS and Linux finds your Balatro, builds the Overhaul from it, finds your saves, backs them up and asks which one to load. Saves export and import as one .zip.
- On Android, the Overhaul Launcher app does the same with the Balatro app from Google Play, with the same tabs as on PC.
- Crash reports are on by default, so problems get looked at faster.
- English, French and Spanish across the whole game.
- The public build deals Balatro's own 150 jokers plus the multiplayer jokers.
