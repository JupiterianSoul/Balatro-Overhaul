# Changelog

What changed for players in each version. The game shows the same notes in English, French and Spanish under About > Changelog.

## 2.0.2

*Overhaul 2.0 update 2, 2026-11-07*

- **Jimbo Jam plays your music.** Every song in your Jukebox shows up in Jimbo Jam, with a chart made from the song itself. Three difficulties, arrow keys on a keyboard.
- **Blue Stake reworked.** Shop items cost $1 more for every Ante cleared and rerolls go up $2 each. No more -1 discard. Every higher stake uses the new rule.
- **Wild Cards.** Wild Cards are immune to every Boss Blind debuff.
- **Matador.** Earn $2 for every discard you use during a Boss Blind.
- **New icons.** The game icon is the original card pair with Hologram and polychrome; the launcher has its own O and L cards.
- Updates are offered on the title screen on every platform, phone installer included, and an open launcher sees the update right away.
- The PC launcher builds the Android APK by itself, with nothing else to install.
- Nova and Eclipse stakes skip the Blue Stake shop tax instead of giving back a discard.

## 2.0.1

*Overhaul 2.0 update, 2026-11-07*

- **Switcheroo fixed.** Real swaps both ways, nothing behind the swap screen, and bots keep playing.
- **Joker Forge.** Colour swatches for card text, no caps on numbers, a tidier Builder, publishing works.
- **Multiplayer decks are back.** Lilac, Seer, Tangerine and Cobalt are back. MP stakes and blinds each have their own look.
- **Replays.** Pause stays paused when you step with the arrows, and names are right.
- **Palettes and packs.** 51 clearly different palettes, nicer Market packs, no blind chip on sleeves.
- Joker Memory shows you the cards for 1 second at the start, and Jimbo Run uses your equipped Jimbo skin.
- The Progression button shows how many rewards are waiting to be claimed.
- Suit colour presets work again, and every suit collab is unlocked for everyone.
- Every trap was checked in real matches; Fire Trap, Tollbooth and Pitfall were fixed.
- A 165 FPS choice in the frame rate cap.
- Touch controls on PCs with a touchscreen (Auto, On or Off in Settings).
- Controller: the D-pad and stick go where you press, and holding A while moving slide-selects cards in your hand.
- A sharp picture on Windows with display scaling, and the CRT effect like vanilla.
- Fixed a crash at the end of a PvP blind while a card was being used.
- Windows launcher: always finds the Steam copy, never blocked by a protected Xbox copy, and adds desktop, Start menu and Steam shortcuts.
- Whack-a-Joker plays well on a controller: a gold frame jumps hole to hole and A hits.
- New Controls > Cursor speed setting for the mouse and the stick cursor.
- The on-screen keyboard always fits on the screen and never covers the field you type in.
- Game speed no longer goes back to 1 after you close the settings.
- Pixel editor: draw with the stick while holding A, and its buttons work with a controller.
- New app icon: the Hologram card, made from your own game files.
- Jimbo Jam, a new minigame: cards fall in four lanes to the game's own music. Two songs, three difficulties, with keys, touch or a controller.
- The Forge Builder works with a controller: paint words in the card text, B closes pickers, and the on-screen keyboard closes properly.
- Linux: the launcher adds Balatro Overhaul and its launcher to your app menu with the new icon. The Android installer has a new icon too.
- **Multiplayer jokers are back.** All 20 MP jokers return, with new looks made from the game's own jokers.
- Manhunt mode is gone.
- Collection > Stakes shows every stake, with Chaos, Apocalypse, Oblivion and Transcendent on their own row.
- Random Deck and the Number randomizer roll much wider, and every copy of a card rolls its own values.
- Clean launch: online profiles start fresh, and old replays are deleted once so every replay matches this version.

## 2.0

*Overhaul 2.0 (pre-release), 2026-11-07*

- **Hologram's tutorial.** Hologram, the new mascot, shows you everything the mod adds. Skip tutorial now skips both.
- **Switcheroo, rebuilt.** Both players press Ready, a 3 second countdown, then you trade everything but lives.
- **Random deck.** Truly random hand scores, prices, card values and boosters, and a Chaos slider in lobbies.
- **Achievements and quests.** Lots of new ones, with pop-ups when you earn them. Quests now live in Progression.
- **The Casino and Cheats.** The Town is now The Casino. Mods are now Cheats and stay off until you allow them in Play.
- What's new window on the title screen, with a Full patch notes button. The About screen is new too.
- The title screen shows an Update button when a new version is out, so you can update from the game itself.
- Bigger UI Size settings no longer push the title screen or windows off the screen.
- Credits: a Special thanks section and a Patreon tab, for those who want to help pay for the server.
- New Stats and Multiplayer Stats windows with more to look at.
- Performance mode rebuilt with clear options, and you can use each option on its own.
- Jimbo's comments are smarter and stay on screen. The Jimbo Advisor is gone.
- The Hand Optimizer was rebuilt on the game's own scoring.
- Shop boosters have their own colours, card backs are more distinct, sleeves sit on the deck like a coat, and the colour palettes are really different from each other.
- The Forge Builder gives you much more freedom.
- Death and other cards with raised values work again above their normal value.
- A blind you already beat can no longer be played again.
- Phone in portrait: Fill the screen widens the run and your hand sits in the middle.
- Custom random draws from almost every run rule.
- Multiplayer balance pass on every mode, trap, joker and blind target.
- The launcher finds your saves, backs them up and asks which one to load. Saves can be exported and imported from Settings.
- Crash reports are on by default, so problems get fixed faster.
- French and Spanish texts reviewed across the whole game.
- The public build ships the 150 Balatro jokers plus the multiplayer jokers.

## test build 2.7

*Portrait mode, round 2, 2026-11-06*

- **Portrait fits.** Cash out and booster packs stay inside the screen, your hand sits higher, and Play, Discard and Sort are bigger.
- **Fill the screen.** New option in Settings > Display for portrait phones: the HUD, shop and cash out use the whole width.
- **Spectate while waiting.** New lobby setting: finish your hands first in a PvP blind and watch the other player live until it ends.
- **Steadier replays.** Replays keep what really happened at every hand and fix themselves if playback drifts. Old replays still play.
- Phone, portrait: Chat and Emote sit between your consumables and your deck, no longer under the Play and Discard buttons.
- Phone, portrait: bigger, easier to read windows everywhere, and the game behind an open window no longer looks stretched.
- Title screen in portrait: no grey band behind the Multiplayer menu, a bigger Alerts button, and the small buttons under Play are bigger.
- The launcher and the in-game Update button can update the Overhaul from the server.

## test build 2.6

*Online fixes and a new launcher, 2026-11-06*

- **Faster online.** Hands, discards and score reach the other players right away: no more lagging panels or lost discards.
- **New launcher.** A launcher in the game's own style. It also finds Balatro from the Xbox app or PC Game Pass.
