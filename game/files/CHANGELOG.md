# Balatro Overhaul changelog

What each version brings to players. The game shows this file in About > Changelog and in the What's new window on the title screen, in English, French or Spanish.

## 2.1.4 | 2026-11-26

### en Overhaul 2.1.4

+ [Performance] Auto Performance (Settings > Performance, on by default): when the game can't hold 60 FPS, it turns effects, card shine, the background and picture sharpness down one step at a time, and back up when there is room. It never goes above your own settings and remembers the level that works for next time.
+ [Performance] Steadier frame rate at full quality: the background swirl redraws 30 times a second with the same picture, and the screen effect and menus do less work every frame. Smooth Background (Settings > Performance) brings the swirl back to 60.
+ [Mods] Modded sessions draw about twice as fast: Steamodded's own screen shader no longer runs under the Overhaul one.
+ [Mods] A mod that changes the Steamodded loading screen no longer crashes the start.
+ [Mods] When several mods break the same file, the error names every mod that could fix it, and the one with the fewest changes there is turned off first.
+ [Gameplay] Blue Stake rework toggle (Settings > Gameplay > Reworks, on by default). Off gives the base game's -1 discard. Daily runs, speedruns and multiplayer keep the standard rules, and the Blue Stake tooltip shows the rules of the run you play.
+ [The Forge] Decks and challenges that start with consumables of your own Card Type start with those cards again instead of Tarots.
+ [Phones] Modded collection pages like Tags open at normal size again.
+ [Multiplayer] Going online now also shares your single-player level and stats, collection progress, mod list, device and performance settings. About > Privacy lists it all.
+ [Fixes] The game no longer stops at start when a language file is broken, and a Mac or Windows install no longer crashes when Steam isn't running.

## 2.1.3 | 2026-11-19

### en Overhaul 2.1.3

+ [The Forge] Easier Forge: Jokers now read as one sentence you can tap to change, long lists have search and groups, you can start from any game card, and a Sample hand tab shows what your Joker scores without starting a run. Numbers that can't go lower now tell you why.
+ [The Forge] Jokers can be animated (their looks play as frames, 1 to 12 per second). Jokers, Tarots, Planets, Spectrals, Vouchers, Boosters and Enhancements can have a floating soul like Legendary Jokers. Every card kind that does something (also Seals, Editions, Tags, Decks and Blinds) can play a sound when it does (a game sound or your own short .ogg, .wav or .mp3).
+ [The Forge] The last vanilla cards can now be rebuilt exactly. New Tarot and Spectral effects: "With a chance" (1 in N, Nope! on a miss), "Make the last Tarot or Planet used" (its hover box shows which card it will make), "Hand to one random suit", "Hand to one random rank", "Copy a random Joker" and "Destroy the other Jokers", plus "Edition on a random Joker" for Tarots with Foil, Holographic or Polychrome at random. Decks get "Balance Chips and Mult" (also Challenges and Stakes) and "Random ranks and suits", and Blinds can show up as a Small Blind or Big Blind (The Fool, The Wheel of Fortune, Sigil, Ouija, Ankh, Hex, Plasma and Erratic Decks, Small and Big Blind).
+ [The Forge] Forged online gets social: Mods > Forged > Browse online has the tabs Featured (a new Forge theme every week, enter up to 3 of your mods, and last week's three best rated lead the list), Best rated, Most played, Newest and Published by me. Rate a mod from 1 to 5 once you have played a run with it (one rating per player, never your own mod), see how many players ran it and how many remixes it has, and press Remix on a mod, online or downloaded, to get its cards in your Forge as your own version, with a credit to the original that stays with it.
+ [The Forge] Creations that use these need Overhaul 2.1.3 or later.

## 2.1.2 | 2026-11-19

### en Overhaul 2.1.2

+ [The Forge] Jokers that grow up: the new effect "Becomes another Joker" turns a Joker into a Joker you pick (from the game, your own creations or a mod) or into a random Joker of a rarity, and it keeps its edition and stickers. Count with Every N times or a variable, like an egg that hatches into a random Rare Joker after 3 rounds.
+ [The Forge] Looks: a Joker can have up to 6 pictures in its new Looks tab. The "Next look" effect moves it to the next one, every Boss beaten for example, or the look can follow a variable.
+ [The Forge] Jokers that boost other Jokers: "Retrigger a Joker" and "A Joker gives more" (double or any amount) for the Joker to the right, to the left, both neighbours or every other Joker, and "A Joker gains Mult, Chips or XMult" for good, for example each round. Works on vanilla, Forge and mod Jokers, and with Steamodded mods like Cryptid's Chad.
+ [The Forge] ^Mult and ^Chips: two new DO steps raise Mult or Chips to a power, like a Legendary that gives ^1.5 Mult on the last hand of a Boss Blind, or ^1.1 Chips for each scored Ace. In Advanced mode they also work everywhere XChips does: enhancements, seals and editions on playing cards, decks, vouchers, stakes and Boss Blinds. With Talisman or Amulet they use its big numbers and colours; without them they stop at 1e100 so the score never shows inf.
+ [The Forge] Your own suits: the new Suit kind (MAKE SOMETHING > Suit) gets an ink colour and a suit symbol you draw in the pixel editor, and turns it into 13 new cards, 2 to Ace. Choose if they are in the starting deck and, in Advanced, how often they show in packs. Flushes of your suit count, Tarots and Spectrals can turn cards into it, Joker rules can check it, and Run Info shows it with its own row and count. Works with and without Steamodded, and travels in mods, online packs and multiplayer rooms.
+ [The Forge] Your own poker hands: the new Poker hand kind picks a pattern (different suits, same suit, same rank, different ranks, in a row, face cards, even or odd ranks), how many cards, which hand it beats, its base Chips and Mult and, in Advanced, its Chips and Mult per level. Make its Planet with a Forge Planet: its Poker hand can now be your own hand. Example: a "Rainbow" (4 cards of different suits, better than a Flush) with its own Planet. Your hands show in Run Info, in Joker rules and in Planets, with and without Steamodded, and travel in mods, online packs and rooms.
+ [The Forge] Your own card types: the new Card type kind gives a family of cards a name, a colour and a shop weight (0 keeps it out of the shop). Tarots and Spectrals pick a Card type to join it, Booster packs can hold a Card type, and each type gets its own page in the Collection. Two new Tarot and Spectral effects change the next Blind: Next Blind size and Disable the next Boss Blind. Examples: "Runes" sold in the shop and in Rune packs, each one changing the next Blind; dark "Curses" found only in their own packs. Works with and without Steamodded, and travels in mods, online packs and rooms.
+ [The Forge] Card types get full rules: a Rules tab for the whole type with WHEN one of these cards is used, bought or sold and every IF and DO (Runes: "$1 each time you use a Rune"), "+$ sell value" for every card of the type, a Negative chance for the ones in shops and packs, and "Most held at once". Jokers can ask "IF it is a Rune" when you use, buy or sell a card, count "for each Rune you have" or "for each Rune used this run", and "Make cards" / "Create a card" can make cards of your own types (Rune Reader: gains +1 Mult when you use a Rune).
+ [The Forge] Your own challenges: the new Challenge kind builds a challenge run without code: starting Jokers (Eternal or not), consumables and vouchers, starting money, hands, discards, hand size and slots, deck tweaks (every card Glass, an edition or a seal, no face cards, remove a rank or a suit, the deck of a game challenge), banned cards, vouchers, pack types, tags and Boss Blinds, no Tarots (or any kind) in the shop, the game's challenge rules (no interest, no reward, Eternal Jokers, face down cards...) and the Forge run rules. It shows up in Play > Challenges (green button). Every game challenge can be opened as a starting point. Example: "Glass Cannon", every card Glass, start with Hanging Chad, no Tarots in the shop. Works with and without Steamodded, and travels in mods, online packs and rooms.
+ [The Forge] Your own rarities and stickers: the new Rarity kind adds a Joker rarity with its own name, colour and shop odds (example: "Epic", pink, 4% of shop Jokers). Jokers pick it in Basics next to Common to Legendary, it shows as their badge, and "Make a Joker" and "Create a Joker by rarity" can make one. The new Sticker kind is a sticker for Jokers like Eternal or Rental: a look, a colour, a chance to come on shop Jokers, and what it does (the Joker scores X2 or more, +Mult, +Chips, XMult, money or sell value every round, can't be sold, destroyed after some rounds). Example: "Cursed", the Joker gives double but costs $2 every round. It is drawn on the Joker with its badge and text, and the new "Put a sticker on a Joker" effect sticks it from a Tarot or Spectral. Works with and without Steamodded, and travels in mods, online packs and rooms.
+ [The Forge] Rarities get full rules: a Rules tab whose WHEN / IF / DO apply to every Joker of the rarity (Epic: "X1.1 Chips when a hand is played" on each Epic Joker), a price range and a sell bonus for its Jokers, and switches for the shop and Buffoon packs. Jokers can ask "IF you have at least 2 Epic Jokers" and count "for each Epic Joker you have" (Epic Fan: X0.5 Mult for each), for the four usual rarities too.
+ [The Forge] Stickers get full rules and go on any card: a Rules tab with every Joker WHEN / IF / DO ("Cursed": X2 Mult when a hand is played, -$2 at round end, on any Joker, vanilla ones too), "Goes on" Jokers, playing cards, consumables or any card ("Golden Touch": +4 Mult and $1 when this card scores), "Lasts rounds" so a sticker peels off ("Fresh": X1.5 Mult for 3 rounds), and the old effects now work on every card. Put stickers from anywhere: the Joker DO "Put a sticker on the card" (Midas Glove: when an Ace scores it gets Golden Touch), "Put a sticker on this Joker", and Decks, Stakes, Challenges, Vouchers, Tags, Tarots and Boss Blinds can put stickers on random Jokers, deck cards or cards in hand, or on more shop cards; Challenges can stick one on the last starting Joker. Jokers can ask "IF the card has a sticker" or "IF you have 2 Jokers with a sticker" and count "for each card with a sticker" (Eternal, Perishable and Rental too).
+ [The Forge] Fusions get much more: a third ingredient ("Trio": Joker + Jolly Joker + Zany Joker make Cavendish), ingredients like "any Joker" or "any Rare Joker" (own rarities too), fusions of consumables ("Rite": Death + The Hanged Man make Cryptid, with "any Tarot", "any Planet" or "any Spectral"), "Fuses by itself" with no button, "Only between rounds", "Sacrifice a Joker" (one more random Joker goes too) and "Keeps upgrades" (stickers and sell value stay, the best edition always stays). A shop card that would complete a fusion shows a "Fusion!" badge with the recipe.
+ [The Forge] Challenges get a starting deck editor and every Deck effect: "Copies of each card" ("Double Deck": 104 cards), "Extra cards of a rank" ("Ace Party": +8 Aces), "Extra cards of a suit" (own suits too), and Glass, seals or editions on N random starting cards, stacking with Remove a rank or suit. Challenges also get the Deck effects they were missing (+hands, +discards, +hand size, +Joker and consumable slots, interest cap, start with a card or with consumables, and more) and Rental or Perishable on the last starting Joker.
+ [The Forge] More moments for your rules: Jokers and the Rules tab of Decks, Stakes, Vouchers, Challenges and Blinds can now react "When you earn money" ("Piggy Bank": +1 Mult each time), "When you spend money" ("Thrift Deck": $1 back), "When a card is drawn" with every card condition ("Card Counter": +5 Chips for each Heart drawn), "When another Joker is sold", "When you get another Joker", "When you get a Tag", "When you beat a Boss Blind" and "When a new ante starts", and Jokers also "When you enter the shop", "When a poker hand levels up" and "When you get a consumable" ("Tarot Lover": $1 for each Tarot). The new IF "The amount" checks how much was earned or spent ("Big Spender": +2 Mult when you spend $5 or more). New counts for "for each" effects: $ earned, $ spent, cards drawn, Jokers sold, Tags gained and Boss Blinds beaten this run ("Big Wallet": +1 Mult for each $ spent this run). Jokers can also debuff a card for N rounds ("Hex": every scored card is debuffed for a round) or make debuffed cards in hand work again ("Medic").
+ [The Forge] Joker fusions: the new Fusion kind is a recipe, two Jokers fuse into a third for a price (from the game or your own creations). When you own both, a Fuse button shows next to Sell; the new Joker keeps the best edition of the two. Examples: Gros Michel + Cavendish = a banana Joker that never goes extinct; two of your own Jokers fused into a third. Works with and without Steamodded, and travels in mods, online packs and rooms.
+ [The Forge] Cards that only show up when it makes sense: "When does it show up?" (Basics of a Joker, Advanced of a Tarot, Planet, Spectral or Booster) now also offers "After a Joker is destroyed", "While you own a Joker" and "After a poker hand is played", next to the vouchers. The card stays out of the shop, packs and tags until then. Examples: a Joker that only shows up after Gros Michel dies (like Cavendish), a Joker that only shows up while you own Cartomancer, a Planet hidden until you have played a Flush once. Works with and without Steamodded, and travels in mods, online packs and rooms.
+ [The Forge] More ways to mess with the run: Jokers (and Enhancements, Seals, Editions) get new effects in their rules: "Blind score x" (lower or raise the Blind's score), "Set the Blind score", "Change the ante" (move it back one), "Level down the hand", "Level up a random hand", "Level up the most played hand" and "Free rerolls" in the shop. Examples: a Joker that halves the Blind when you select it, a Joker that moves the ante back one at the end of the round, a Joker that gives a free reroll in every shop. In Simple and Advanced, with and without Steamodded, and they travel in mods, online packs and rooms.
+ [The Forge] Shop Jokers: "While you have this Joker" now also offers "Shop card slots" (an extra card in the shop), "Cheaper rerolls" (rerolls cost $1 less) and "Free card each shop" (the first card of every shop is free). Example: a Bargain Hunter Joker with all three. Works with and without Steamodded, and travels in mods, online packs and rooms.
+ [The Forge] Special effect: in the pixel editor, give a Joker, Tarot, Planet, Spectral, Voucher, Booster pack, Enhancement or Seal the look of Foil, Holographic, Polychrome, Negative, one of your own editions or a mod edition. It is only a look and changes nothing in the game. If the card later gets a real edition, like Negative from Perkeo, both show and the real edition works as usual.
+ [The Forge] Every "for each" gain now has its losing twin: "Lose money for each", "-Chips for each" and "-Mult for each" (counting things like your Jokers or discards left), and the same three "for each point" of a variable. They work in Otherwise too, like "earn $1 for each Joker, otherwise lose $2 for each Joker".
+ [The Forge] How many cards you can select: choose more or fewer than 5 (never below 1), for playing and discarding. "Cards to select" while you have a Joker (Big Hands: +2), "Cards to select for good" or "for one round" as a DO in any rule, Decks and Challenges that start with any number (Wide Deck: 7), Vouchers, Stakes, Tarots, Spectrals and Tags that add or remove some, and Boss Blinds where "You can only select 3 cards" (Tight Grip). With 6 or more cards, Flush, Straight, Straight Flush, Full House, Two Pair and the rest still find the best hand, and the new "Every suit" pattern makes poker hands like Rainbow Flood (7 cards with every suit).
+ [The Forge] Suits get full rules: a Rules tab like Enhancements (when scored, held in hand, discarded or held at end of round, with every IF and DO; Stars: "when scored, +5 Mult", Moons: "$1 when held at end of round"), "Also counts as" one or two other suits (Stars count as Hearts and Spades for Flushes, Jokers and Boss Blinds), +Chips, +Mult, XMult and $ when scored, XMult while held and $ at end of round, a rank range (Royals: only Jack to King), any number of copies of each rank in the starting deck (type it, up to 1000), "Never debuffed" and the Boss Blind that debuffs it. Your suits now show in every suit choice: "for each Stars card in your deck", "the hand has a scoring Stars card", "every held card is Stars", Deck and Challenge "Remove a suit", and Boss "Debuff a suit" (that one now really debuffs your suits). Hover a card of your suit to see what it does.
+ [The Forge] Your own creations can now be picked everywhere: own Boss Blinds, poker hands, suits, rarities, card types, stickers and stakes show up in every list that picks that kind of thing (Level up a hand, shop weights, rarity weights, "Blind is" on a Joker, "Debuffed by" on a Suit, "Better than" on a hand, "On top of" on a Stake, "Becomes" and more). Decks get "Add a suit" (your own suits too), "Every card has a seal", "Every card has an edition" and "Remove a rank", there is a "Remove a sticker" for Jokers, cards and runs, Eternal, Perishable and Rental can be put on Jokers, and "Choose the next Boss Blind" picks the next Boss (your own ones too). Challenges can ban your own Boss Blinds and packs (or every pack of one of your card types), keep one of your card types out of the shop, put your own enhancements, seals and editions on every card or on the starting Joker, copy the deck of another of your challenges, and a new "Deck" effect plays the challenge on any deck, your own decks with all their effects too.
+ [The Forge] Poker hands have no card limit any more: "Cards" goes past 5 (type any number), so a hand can need 6, 7 or more cards for Jokers or decks that let you select more.

Una temporada cada tres meses, con un reinicio parcial, y una insignia y una funda de temporada para tu mejor rango. La XP de clasificatoria abre sobres de títulos, fundas, marcos de cartas y comodines, llamas, explosiones y celebraciones.
+ [Diario y Semanal] Un desafío multijugador con reglas, baraja y semilla elegidas por el servidor, uno o dos modificadores al azar, y una clasificación.
+ [Ajustes de sala] El anfitrión elige las vidas, la apuesta en la que empieza el PvP, la apuesta final, la baraja (o deja que cada uno elija), la apuesta, la semilla, los temporizadores, los tiempos muertos gratis, el robo de vida, un premio por ganar el PvP, dinero de remontada, la puntuación rival oculta, una cola de tienda compartida, las cartas prohibidas y cualquier regla de partida personalizada. 3 huecos para guardar ajustes.
+ [Reglas listas] Reglas ya hechas para cada modo: Clásico, Equilibrado, Vanilla, Fuego lento, Torneo, Escasez, Sprint, Caos, Duelo rápido, Fuego rápido, Maratón, Cañón de cristal, Muerte súbita, Baño de sangre, Sin cargar y Heroico.
+ [Modificadores] Capas que el anfitrión puede sumar: tres velocidades de reloj, cartas de cristal resistentes o frágiles, Inflación, Sin intereses, Impuesto de descarte, Ahorrador, Espartano, tienda más grande o más pequeña, Precios misteriosos, Bolsillos compartidos, Puntuación inestable, Sin sellos rojos, Sin apuestas, Escasez, Cartas boca abajo y Comodines eternos.
+ [Trampas] Los Sobres trampa de la tienda te dejan poner una de 12 trampas ocultas al rival. Por ejemplo, Peaje hace que sus cambios de tienda te paguen, y Cepo convierte en Alquiler el próximo comodín que compre. Hasta 3 trampas pueden esperar a un mismo jugador.
+ [Comodines multijugador] 20 comodines que solo existen en multijugador, como Tregua, Rencor, Duelo espejo y Gran apostador, y comodines hechos para Ascensión, Carrera, Battle Royale, Equipos y Raid. Llevan un tinte morado y una pequeña marca MP en la esquina.
+ [Barajas y apuestas] Las barajas Lila, Vidente, Mandarina, Cobalto, Géminis y Aleatoria. La baraja Aleatoria da fichas y multi al azar a cada mano y mezcla precios, valores de cartas y sobres, con un control de caos. Tres apuestas multijugador: Nova, Eclipse y Eclipse+.
+ [Rediseños] Algunas reglas cambian unas cuantas cartas base: Voto colgante, Heliotropo, cartas de cristal, Boleto dorado, Soda, Judía tortuga y Ouija. La sala y Colección > Rediseños muestran cuáles.
+ [Amigos y chat] Lista de amigos con estado en línea, invitaciones y Pedir unirse, mensajes privados, chat de sala que sigue funcionando en la partida, 12 emotes, silenciar, denunciar y bloquear. El chat pregunta tu edad una vez y es para mayores de 13 años. Los cosméticos repetidos se pueden regalar a amigos.
+ [Repeticiones y espectador] Las partidas terminadas se guardan como repeticiones con pausa, velocidad y salto. Mira en directo partidas públicas o de tus amigos, o mira a los demás cuando hayas jugado tus manos.
+ [Bots y salas con mods] El anfitrión puede añadir bots Fácil, Medio o Difícil. Las salas pueden usar mods (beta): muestran sus mods y los que faltan se instalan al unirte.
+ [4 apuestas nuevas] Después de la apuesta dorada. Caos: los sobres ofrecen una opción menos. Apocalipsis: las ciegas jefe también debilitan un palo al azar. Olvido: un comodín al azar queda debilitado en cada ciega. Trascendencia: todas las ciegas jefe son finales. Cada una mantiene las anteriores, sube en uno la apuesta para ganar (de 10 a 13) y tiene su propia pegatina.
+ [Partidas personalizadas] Tus propias reglas en 8 categorías: básicas, economía, ciegas, tienda, baraja, objetos, prohibidos y diversión. Manos, descartes, tamaño de mano, huecos, dinero, intereses, tamaño de las ciegas, probabilidades de la tienda, baraja de 1 a 208 cartas, comodines y vales iniciales, cartas prohibidas, un aleatorizador de números y más. Guarda tus reglas como preajustes.
+ [Partidas aleatorias] Una baraja y una apuesta al azar, o una Partida personalizada aleatoria con reglas al azar que puedes leer antes de empezar.
+ [Partida diaria] La misma semilla para todos cada día, con 2 giros, 1 ventaja y un comodín inicial. Un intento puntuado al día, una racha y una clasificación con pestaña de Amigos.
+ [Speedruns] Categorías Apuesta 2, Apuesta 4 y Any%, con una semilla semanal o al azar. Un cronómetro con parciales por apuesta, récords personales, una clasificación en línea y repeticiones de las mejores partidas.
+ [Desafíos nuevos] 10 desafíos nuevos: Bancarrota, El Guantelete, Manos pequeñas, Pegador, Cañón de cristal, El Coleccionista, Comodín, Contrarreloj, El Alquimista y Dualidad.
+ [Práctica de jefes] Enfréntate a cualquier jefe que ya hayas visto, con la baraja y el equipo que quieras, incluso sacado de una partida anterior. Sin premios, y nada cuenta en tus estadísticas.
+ [Historial] Tus últimas 200 partidas con su semilla, baraja, apuesta, mejor mano y comodines finales. Vuelve a jugar la semilla o mira la repetición de cualquiera de tus últimas 50 partidas.
+ [Biblioteca de códigos] Guarda semillas con su baraja y apuesta, o pega una, y juégalas más tarde.
+ [Creador de barajas] Crea tus propias barajas: reglas, dinero, probabilidades de la tienda, las cartas exactas de la baraja, objetos iniciales y giros. Compártelas con un código de texto.
+ [Trucos] Actívalos antes de una partida y usa el botón Trucos (se puede mover) para añadir comodines, cartas, consumibles y vales, subir manos, cambiar dinero, manos y huecos, o ganar la ciega. Una partida con trucos no da progreso.
+ [Estadísticas] Estadísticas en solitario y multijugador: victorias, rachas, mejor mano, apuesta más alta, tiempo jugado, resultados por baraja y apuesta, y tus comodines y manos favoritos.
+ [Mercado] Gasta Balacoins en sobres de cosméticos: sobres Paleta, Bufón, Funda, Brillo y Título, sobres Jumbo, y las Cajas de la Taberna y Legendarias. Todas las probabilidades están en la pestaña Probabilidades, lo que no tienes sale 3 veces más, los repetidos vuelven a ser Balacoins, y las Cajas legendarias dan un Legendario al menos cada 40 cartas.
+ [Sastre] Equipa tus cosméticos: icono de perfil y su brillo, título, paletas de colores, tapete, dorsos de carta en 5 acabados, llamas de puntuación, celebraciones, retratos y aspectos de Jimbo. La paleta Policromática tiene su propio editor: elige cada color por tono, saturación y brillo y míralo en directo. Nueva paleta legendaria en el Mercado: Chicle.
+ [Arcade] 10 minijuegos: Mayor o menor, Memoria de comodines, Nombra la mano, Encuentra el as, Blackjack, Golpea al comodín, Tragaperras, Vence a la ciega, Jimbo Run y Jimbo Jam, un juego de ritmo que puede tocar cualquier canción de tu Jukebox. Cada uno guarda tu mejor puntuación, y puedes jugarlos mientras esperas en multijugador.
+ [Dentro de la Forja] Las reglas de los comodines se crean con 20 activadores, 23 condiciones y 26 efectos, con variables. El editor de píxeles tiene herramientas de dibujo, formas y selección, una capa de alma e importación de imágenes. Exporta tus creaciones como archivo .forgemod, publícalas en línea o haz que toda tu sala juegue con ellas.
+ [Misiones y logros] 4 misiones diarias (un cambio gratis al día), 4 semanales, y 24 Trayectos de 3 a 5 etapas. Los logros tienen avisos, rarezas, y dan títulos, insignias y cosméticos.
+ [Normal y Con mods] Dos juegos separados, que se cambian desde la pantalla de título. Normal nunca carga mods, Con mods carga los tuyos. Cada uno tiene sus 3 perfiles, así que los mods no tocan tus partidas principales.
+ [Mods] La mayoría de los mods de Steamodded y Lovely funcionan, también en Android. El menú Mods los activa, desactiva, abre sus ajustes, los actualiza y los quita. Un .zip de mod en Descargas se instala al iniciar. Si un mod cuelga el juego al iniciar, el siguiente inicio lo desactiva y te dice cuál fue.
+ [Workshop] Explora, busca e instala mods del Balatro Mod Index sin salir del juego. Los mods necesarios se instalan solos, y cada mod indica si funciona en tu plataforma.

+ [Lanzador] Para Windows, macOS, Linux y Android. Encuentra tu Balatro (Steam, la app de Xbox, Flatpak), crea el Overhaul a partir de él, trae tu progreso de Balatro o empieza de cero, hace copias de tus partidas y añade accesos directos. En PC también puede crear una app de Android desde tu copia de Steam. En Android crea una segunda app, Balatro Overhaul, que se abre directamente en el juego, se actualiza sola y se lleva tus partidas.
+ [Actualizaciones y partidas] El juego y el lanzador te avisan de una actualización y la instalan, y tus partidas se quedan. Exporta todos tus perfiles en un solo .zip e impórtalos en otro dispositivo. Se hace una copia antes de cada importación.
+ [Controles] Soporte completo de mando con reasignación y selección deslizando, una partida entera jugable con teclado y reasignación, táctil en PC y móvil, un modo panel táctil y un ajuste de velocidad del cursor.
+ [Ayudas] Vista previa de la puntuación, información bajo cada comodín, Jimbo comentando tu partida, seleccionar arrastrando, valor de venta total, reinicio rápido, protección de intereses, venta segura, sobres rápidos, una tecla de velocidad hasta 20x en solitario, teclas de silencio, pausa al cambiar de ventana y búsqueda en los ajustes.
+ [Jukebox] Pon tu propia música en lugar de la banda sonora. Mete archivos mp3, ogg, wav o flac en la carpeta Mi música, o deja que el juego los busque en Descargas.
+ [Pantalla y rendimiento] Un modo rendimiento, y opciones separadas para nitidez, fondo, brillo de cartas, partículas y fotogramas por segundo. Filtros de color, contador de FPS, tamaño de la interfaz y Llenar la pantalla en el móvil en vertical.
