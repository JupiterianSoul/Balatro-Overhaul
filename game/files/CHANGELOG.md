# Balatro Overhaul changelog

What each version brings to players. The game shows this file in About > Changelog and in the What's new window on the title screen, in English, French or Spanish.

## 2.0.6 | 2026-11-13

### en Overhaul 2.0.6
+ [Multiplayer] New pick screen when the match starts: with Each player picks a deck or Starting joker picks on, everyone, host included, picks their deck and up to 5 Common or Uncommon jokers, again before every rematch. With Deck draft on, you pick from the 3 decks left after the bans.
+ [Multiplayer] Bots take part too: they ban decks in Deck draft, get a random deck and random starting jokers by the same rules as players, and really play them.
+ [Multiplayer] Lost your connection? Rejoin within 15 minutes and the match picks up where it was.
+ [Mods] One Mods switch replaces the PLAY WITH MODS and MODDED COLLECTION buttons: flip it, press Restart now, and the line above the button says why a restart is needed. Turning mods off keeps your ticked mods for next time.
+ [Mods] A MODDED or NORMAL badge shows under the online status on the title and during a run. Tap it on the title to open the Mods screen.
+ [Mods] Rejoining a modded match after unticking your mods turns them back on and puts you straight back in the match.
+ [Multiplayer] New lobby options: Random pick for the deck, Random for the stake, and Boss effect on PvP, so the PvP blind also carries a real boss effect.
+ [Everything else] New Run: tap the deck or the stake to see them all in a grid and pick one.
+ [Launcher] From this version on, the launcher updates itself: a new launcher downloads in the background and is used after RESTART or the next start. On phones it also offers UPDATE THE LAUNCHER when the app itself needs an update.
+ [Launcher] On Windows, updating from the in-game Update button no longer fails with "Could not write ... Is the disk full?".
+ [Phones] With auto-rotate off, the game follows your phone's locked orientation instead of going back to landscape, and Screen Orientation set to Portrait or Landscape always wins (press REBUILD THE APP once).
+ [Phones] New Gameplay setting, PC-style selection: tap a joker or consumable to select it, so cards that target a selected joker work (Cryptid's Code cards for example).
+ [Tailor] Every category has a Random card: a new owned look is picked at the start of each game and each multiplayer match. With Random on, tap cards to ban them from the rotation.
+ [The Forge] Jokers and other creations can create a Random Tag, rolled each time the effect fires.
+ [Everything else] New setting to pick the mouse button for Drag to Select (right or left). With left, start the drag on empty space; clicking and moving cards work as before.
+ [Multiplayer] Bots never leave a match anymore, and they think at normal speed again: no more minute-long turns, and Medium bots shop properly.
+ [Multiplayer] The PvP countdown works again, Final two sudden death only starts after a knockout, and every lobby setting now says exactly what it does.
+ [Multiplayer] Pop-ups don't hide behind the title anymore, a closed room offers you a new one, and rooms take up to 50 Forge mods.
+ [Multiplayer] The mods warning only shows when you really have mods on, the button to turn them off and join works, and a room whose host left says so instead of "wrong code".
+ [Phones] Small windows are as big in portrait as in landscape, and the last screens that got squeezed or cut after turning the phone are fixed.
+ [Phones] The run HUD grows with UI size above 100 when the screen has room.
+ [Phones] Achievement, unlock, level and quest pop-ups are the same size in portrait as in landscape.
+ [Phones] The deck count table no longer gets stuck over your hand.
+ [Phones] A joker lifted with a pen or a mouse no longer stays floating: tap it to put it back, even after reloading.
+ [Phones] On an app made by an older launcher, Screen Orientation and the file picker say what to do: update the Overhaul Launcher, then press REBUILD THE APP.
+ [Phones] If the launcher's screen effect turns your screen black, it now switches itself off, and the launcher can make the app on every Balatro version again.
+ [The Forge] Hold the Starts at arrows to go faster, effect pages fill every row, and the score preview counts voucher and deck bonuses.
+ [Mods] Talisman and Amulet start about twice as fast, and no longer crash the shop or the sound.
+ [Mods] Cryptid's Overstock Plus, Antimatter and Tycoon vouchers work again, and the Antimatter and Equilibrium decks no longer crash at the start.
+ [Mods] Continuing a run after turning a mod on or off no longer crashes later on.
+ [Mods] Big hands and mods that keep rolling for luck no longer freeze the game.
+ [Mods] Boss Blinds show their description again on the blind select screen and in Run Info when mods are on.
+ [Mods] Restart now restarts the game even while Workshop thumbnails or downloads are still loading, instead of closing it.
+ [Mods] When no mod can start, the game starts as the normal game and says why, and a long "needs" message on a mod no longer shrinks to unreadable text.
+ [Mods] Multiplayer blinds no longer show up as bosses in singleplayer runs with mods on.
+ [Mods] The Collection shows Tarot, Planet and Spectral cards again with mods on.
+ [Mods] Cartomancer no longer hides the MULTIPLAYER text on the main menu.
+ [Mods] Bunco no longer flips the stake list: a new profile starts on White Stake and stakes unlock one by one. New Run and Random also reach every stake a mod adds.
+ [Multiplayer] Play while you wait no longer covers the Opponent disconnected box in portrait.
+ [Everything else] No more stray Play/Discard label on the main menu, French texts fit, and about 15 crash fixes from your reports.

## 2.0.5 | 2026-11-12

### en Overhaul 2.0.5
+ [Phones] Portrait and landscape: every screen works and reads well both ways, and turning your phone keeps you where you were. If auto-rotate acts up, pick one in Settings > Display > Screen Orientation.

+ [Mods] Mods can't mess with files outside the game anymore, and you're asked before turning on one that isn't from the Workshop.
+ [Mods] If a mod breaks the game at start, it tells you which one and only turns that one off.
+ [Mods] Cryptid, Paperback and other popular mods crash less, and switching mods on or off mid-run is fine now.
+ [Everything else] Replay controls stay out of the way, and you get a proper end screen when it's over.
+ [Everything else] A smarter Bot-latro, and Easy actually makes mistakes again.
+ [Everything else] Matador is back to the original, and Blue Stake now raises shop prices instead of taking a discard.
+ [Everything else] You see what changed after each update, and there are Discord and GitHub buttons on the menu.
+ [Everything else] The Tailor works with taps again, Workshop downloads retry on their own, and around 40 crash fixes.

En Android crea una segunda app, Balatro Overhaul, que se abre directamente en el juego, se actualiza sola y se lleva tus partidas.
+ [Actualizaciones y partidas] El juego y el lanzador te avisan de una actualización y la instalan, y tus partidas se quedan. Exporta todos tus perfiles en un solo .zip e impórtalos en otro dispositivo. Se hace una copia antes de cada importación.
+ [Controles] Soporte completo de mando con reasignación y selección deslizando, una partida entera jugable con teclado y reasignación, táctil en PC y móvil, un modo panel táctil y un ajuste de velocidad del cursor.
+ [Ayudas] Vista previa de la puntuación, información bajo cada comodín, Jimbo comentando tu partida, seleccionar arrastrando, valor de venta total, reinicio rápido, protección de intereses, venta segura, sobres rápidos, una tecla de velocidad hasta 20x en solitario, teclas de silencio, pausa al cambiar de ventana y búsqueda en los ajustes.
+ [Jukebox] Pon tu propia música en lugar de la banda sonora. Mete archivos mp3, ogg, wav o flac en la carpeta Mi música, o deja que el juego los busque en Descargas.
+ [Pantalla y rendimiento] Un modo rendimiento, y opciones separadas para nitidez, fondo, brillo de cartas, partículas y fotogramas por segundo. Filtros de color, contador de FPS, tamaño de la interfaz y Llenar la pantalla en el móvil en vertical.
+ [Idiomas] Todo el Overhaul está en inglés, francés y español.
